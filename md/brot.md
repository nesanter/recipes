# Brot

## Ingredients

 - 615g bread flour
 - 11g sugar
 - 15g salt
 - 2¼ tsp yeast
 - 380g 100°F water
 - corn meal on baking tray

## Instructions

 - 8 minute knead with dough hook
 - 2 hour rise in oven with light on, in plastic-wrapped bowl.
 - 45 minute proof on baking tray with parchment, covered in dampened towel
   (while the oven and bread pot preheat to 450°F)
 - Dust preheated pot with cornmeal. Add bread. Cut cross shape with lame.
 - 25 minute bake with the lid
 - 5 to 10 minute bake with the lid off
