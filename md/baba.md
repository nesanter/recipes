# Baba with Beef Filling

## Ingredients

### Baba

 - ⅓  cup yogurt
 - 1 tsp yeast
 - 1¾ cup water, lukewarm
 - 2 cups and 3 cups of flour
 - Pinch salt

### Beef Filling

 - ¼ cup chili oil
 - 1 lb beef
 - Pinch salt
 - 1 tbsp black beans
 - ~1 tbsp light soy sauce
 - 1 tbsp szechuan peppercorns, ground
 - Scallions, thinly sliced

## Instructions

### Baba

 1. Combine water, yoghurt, yeast, salt, and 2 cups of flour. Stir until
    smooth. Cover and let rise 1 hour.

 2. Add remaining flour until dough is not too sticky. Knead with hook for
    about ten minutes. Round into ball and let rise 2 hours.

 3. Make filling and chop scallions.

 4. If the dough is in the oven, remove it. OR ELSE. Preheat oven to 300°F.
    (You removed it, right?)

 5. Divide into 4 pieces. Roll each piece out into an oval and put filling
    on it, avoiding about 1" around each side. Top with scallions. Roll it up,
    pinching the edges as you go. Turn the cylinder on its side and push down
    gently. Roll out again.

 6. Preheat a skillet and cook the rounds 6 minutes per side on low. Pan might
    get a bit hot and need some cooling with water between rounds.

 7. Finish each round by wrapping in foil and baking for 20 minutes.

### Filling

 1. Heat chili oil. Add beef and salt and break up.

 2. After it has browned, add beans and mix. At the end, add peppercorns and
    soy sauce. Simmer off the soy sauce briefly and set filling aside.
