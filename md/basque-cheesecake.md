# Basque Cheesecake

## Ingredients

 - 750g cream cheese
 - 200g sugar
 - 1¼ cup heavy cream
 - 30g flour
 - 1 to 2 tsp vanilla
 - 220g egg, lightly beaten

## Instructions

 1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Crumple and press parchment paper into springform.

 2. Paddle cream cheese 2 minutes on medium. Incorporate sugar until just
    dissolved.

 3. Whisk ¼ cup of the cream into the flour to form a paste. Whisk in remaining
    cream and vanilla.

 4. On low, slowly add cream into cream cheese. Add eggs without overbeating.

 5. Pour into prepared pan. Pop bubbles. Bake 45 min (up to 65) until top is
    deeply golden.

 6. Cool two hours. Refrigerate overnight, plus up to 5 days after. Be careful
    to manage condensation in the fridge.
