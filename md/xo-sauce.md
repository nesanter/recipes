# XO Sauce

## Ingredients

 - 150g (5.5oz) dried scallops
 - 150g (5.5oz) dried shrimp
 - 5 tbsp Shaoxing wine, divided into 1 tbsp, 1 tbsp, and 3 tbsp
 - 7 shallots
 - 1½ heads garlic
 - 5 fresh red chili peppers
 - 100g (3.5oz) Jinhua ham
 - ¾ cup chicken stock
 - 1 tbsp brown sugar
 - 3½ cups oil
 - 1 tbsp dark mushroom soy sauce
 - 2 tbsp fish sauce
 - ¼ cup dried chili flakes

## Instructions

 1. Put dried shrimp and dried scallops each in their own bowl. Add 1 tbsp of
    Shaoxing wine to each, and then cover with boiling water. Soak for 2 to
    six hours.

 2. Use food processor to mince shallots and garlic into one bowl and set
    aside. Then, use food processor to mince the chilies into another bowl and
    set aside. Break up the ham.

 3. Drain the seafood bowls and steam each for 15 minutes, collecting the
    extra liquid from the scallops and setting aside. Let cool. Pulse the
    scallops in the food processor until they are short threads. Set aside.
    Then, pulse the shrimp until they are fine crumbs.

 4. Mix the scallop liquid with the chicken stock and brown sugar. Set aside.

 5. Heat oil to 225°F. Fry the scallops for 5 minutes, then add the shrimp and
    fry both for 15 minutes. Add the ham and cook for 3 minutes. Add the
    chilies and cook for 3 minutes. Turn off the heat, and strain, keeping the
    oil. Set aside the seafood mixture into another pan.

 6. Heat the oil back to 225°F. Add the shallots and fry for 10 minutes.

 7. While you do this, heat the fried seafood togeter with the chicken stock
    mixture, 3 tbsp of Shaoxing wine, and the dark mushrooom soy sauce. Stir
    to combine and simmer.

 8. Add the garlic to the hot oil. Cook it for 10 minutes. When the shallots
    and garlic are golden, re-add (carefully) the seafood mixture into the
    hot wok. Add the fish sauce and chili flakes.

 9. Put the final result in a clean container and let it cool before
    refrigerating.
