# Oxtail Pie

## Ingredients

## Filling

 - 2lb oxtail with bones
 - Beef stock
 - butter
 - flour
 - 2-3 good, big carrots
 - mushrooms
 - dried chilies (e.g. guajillo)
 - salt
 - black pepper

## Egg Wash

 - Egg
 - Water

## Crust

 - 300g flour
 - 1 to 1½ tsp salt
 - 2 sticks butter
 - ½ cup ice water

## Instructions

 1. Preheat oven to 300°F.

 2. Sweat chopped mushrooms in butter, salt, and pepper with chilies. Add
    carrots, cut into nice pieces. Sprinkle on ~2 tbsp of flour.

 3. Add oxtails and stock most of the way up the meat. Bring to a simmer.

 4. Bake for 2 to 4 hours, until the liquid is almost completely reduced and
    the meat is extremely tender.

 5. Put together crusts, allowing 30 minutes to chill before rolling.

 6. Remove oxtails and cool enough to remove meat from bones. Combine together,
    adding more stock if needed until it is thick and gel-y.

 7. Put between two pie crusts (no need to blind bake) and apply an egg wash
    to the top. Vent. Bake on a sheet tray (seriously) at 350°F for 40 minutes
    to an hour.

 8. Let sit at least one hour to set.
