# Mexican Red Rice

## Ingredients

 - 2 cup long grain rice
 - 1 cup plus 2 cup water
 - 2 tomatoes
 - ½ onion
 - tomato paste
 - chicken BTB
 - salt
 - oil
 - 1 jalapeño

## Instructions

 1. Blend 1 cup water, chopped tomatoes and onion, tomato paste, BTB, and salt.

 2. Rinse the dry rice. Heat a few tbsp of oil. Fry the rinsed rice for about
    5 minutes. Add remaining water. Press blended salsa through a strainer.
    Bring to a simmer. Top with halved jalapeño.

 3. Cook on lowest low for 20 minutes, covered.

 4. Let sit ten minutes.
