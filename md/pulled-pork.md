# Pulled Pork Sandwiches

Makes about 6 burger-bun sized sandwiches. (I like the St. Pierre brioche
buns for this.)

## Ingredients

 - half a bag of shredded cole slaw mix
 - 3 to 4 cups or so white vinegar
 - sugar
 - ⅓ cup or so mayo

 - chipotle, ancho, and pasilla chilis
 - an onion
 - 4 to 6 cloves of garlic, peeled
 - ¼ cup brown sugar
 - 1 tbsp beef BTB
 - a few tbsp tomato paste
 - some neutral oil
 - 2 pork tenderloins (about 1½lb)
 - ½ to ¾ cup ketchup
 - 6 buns
 - a few tbsp butter

## Instructions

### Cole Slaw

 1. The night before, combine vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small pot. Bring
    to a boil and pour over a large deli container's worth of slaw mix.

 2. Refrigerate overnight.

 3. Shortly before serving, drain the liquid. Add some mayo (about a third of
    a cup or so), put the lid back on the container, and shake it until
    combined.

### Pork

 1. Preheat oven to 300°F.

 2. Seed (don't go crazy, just as well as you can) and de-stem a mix of five or
    six chipotles, four to six anchos, and four to six pasillas. Put in a medium
    pot with 1 to 2 tbsp or so of salt and a roughly chopped onion. Add water
    to cover and bring to a boil.

 3. Take the chili-onion mix off heat and use a strainer to drain most of the
    liquid while retaining it in case it is required in the recipe ahead.

 4. With enough liquid so it will blend, blend the chili-onion mix. Add garlic
    cloves, brown sugar, beef BTB, and tomato paste and blend until smooth.

 5. In a dutch oven, heat some oil. When it's hot, pour in the blended sauce.
    It should bubble. Stir it together and put in the pork. Add any reserved
    liquid to get the pork nearly covered, and spoon chili mix over all parts
    of the pork.

 6. Bake uncovered for three hours.

 7. Take the pork out of the chili and let it cool slightly. Mix spoonfuls of
    the baked chili sauce with ketchup to form barbecue sauce. Add enough for
    it to taste how you want.

 8. Cut up (against the grain) the pork every ¾” or so. Cut down the slices
    a few times into chunks. Put the cut meat into a bowl and shred it (use a
    stirring motion with a fork).

 9. Add ¼ to ½ cup of chili sauce to the meat bowl and stir to cover the pork.

### Assembly

 1. Toast buns with a bit of butter. Assemble sandwich with shredded pork,
    barbecue sauce, and prepared cole slaw.
