# Mincemeat Pie

## Ingredients

### Mincemeat

 - ½ to ¾ cup candied orange peel (succade)
 - ½ cup raisins
 - ½ cup golden raisings
 - 2 granny smith apples
 - 1 small bag fresh cranberries
 - ⅔ cup dark brown sugar
 - ⅓ cup white sugar
 - 1 tsp cinnamon
 - 1 tsp allspice
 - 1 tsp mace blades
 - 1 stick butter
 - ⅔ plus ⅓ cup brandy
 - ⅓ cup water

### Crust

## Instructions

 1. While the succade is being made, combine everything except ⅓ cup brandy.

 2. Cook at a medium-lower simmer, covered until the cranberries have released
    their water, then uncovered.

 3. Add the succade once it's done and keep cooking, for a total cook time of
    about one hour. Stir frequently towards the end.

 4. Off the heat, add the final ⅓ cup brandy and let cool.

 5. Roll out a bottom crust for a tart pan and blind bake it for 15 minutes.

 6. Let it air out a bit and roll out the top crust, then fill the pie and
    bake for 30 to 45 minutes. Let cool 15-20 minutes before slicing.
