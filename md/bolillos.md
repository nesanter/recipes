# Bolillos

## Ingredients

 - 1 packet yeast
 - 4 cup bread flour
 - 1 tsp salt
 - 1 tsp sugar
 - 1⅓ cup warm water

## Instructions

 1. Combine and knead briefly with dough hook, adding extra water if needed.

 2. Let rise in a greased bowl for 1 hour.

 3. Deflate and knead for ten minutes.

 4. Divide into 10 balls. Shape as needed. Score with lame. Cover and let proof
    30 minutes.

 5. Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes with steam.
