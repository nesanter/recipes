# Char Siu Bao

See separate char siu recipe for preparing the meat, which can be done the
day before to let it cool and firm up in the fridge.

Makes 16 bao.

## Ingredients

### Dough

 - 470g flour
 - 100g cornstarch
 - 10g baking powder
 - 6g yeast
 - 100g sugar
 - 9 oz water
 - 1 oz (2 tbsp) vegetable oil

### Filling

 - 1 lb char siu, chopped into small pieces
 - 1 oz oyster sauce
 - ½ oz light soy sauce
 - ½ oz dark soy sauce
 - 16g sugar
 - 0.25g five spice powder
 - 2 clove garlic, microplaned
 - 2 tsp sesame oil
 - 1 tsp cornstarch
 - 2 oz water

## Instructions

 1. Mix dry dough ingredients thoroughly. With mixer on low, slowly add water
    and vegetable oil. Knead with dough hook for 8 minutes.

 2. Shape into ball. Let rise in greased bowl, covered with a towel, for 2
    hours.

 3. Chop char siu into small pieces.

 4. In a saucepan, combine filling ingredients except char siu, cornstarch, and
    water. Bring to a simmer. Let thicken if needed. Add cornstarch slurry.
    Let thicken then add char siu and mix to coat. Set filling aside.

 5. Degas dough. Weigh it to determine how much each sixteenth will weight.
    Roll into a log and cut into pieces, measuring each piece until they are
    all roughly even.

 6. Take a piece and roll out into a 6" diameter circle. Add about 2 tbsp in
    the center. Pleat and twist off, then set aside until a damp towel. Repeat
    with the remaining pieces.

 7. Let bao proof for 15 minutes. Near the end of the proofing, bring water in
    your steamer to a boil and preheat the steamer tray.

 8. Steam about 3 bao at once, leaving room for expansion, for 10 minutes.
