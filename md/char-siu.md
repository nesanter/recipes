# Char Siu

1.5lb of raw pork produces roughly enough char siu for one batch of 16 bao,
which calls for 1lb of cooked char siu.

## Ingredients

 - 1½ lb pork butt or tenderloin
 - 50g sugar
 - 56g fermented red bean curd sauce
 - 20g fermented red bean curd curd
 - 81g honey
 - 32g dark soy sauce
 - 45g molasses
 - 10g salt
 - 12g garlic powder
 - 1.5g five spice powder
 - 3 tbsp Shaoxing wine

## Instructions

 1. Combine all ingredients into marinade. Reserve some marinade for later
    brushing. Cover the meat thoroughly with the remainder. Marinade overnight,
    turning halfway through.

 2. Bring meat up to room temperature for 30 minutes.

 3. Preheat oven to 375°F. Warm remaining marinade in microwave to loosen.

 4. Place meat on sheet pan and add water to bottom of pan. Bake for 20 minutes.

 5. Brush meat, turn it, and brush it again. Bake for 15 minutes.

 6. Raise heat to 425°F. Bake for 5 minutes.

 7. Remove from oven, brush one final time, and let cool before cutting.
