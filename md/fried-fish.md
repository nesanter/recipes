# Fried Fish

## Ingredients

(per 1lb of fish)

 - 75g + 25g flour
 - 75g + 25g rice flour (mochiko)
 - 4g salt
 - 2g baking powder
 - 6 to 8oz beer

## Instructions

 1. Dry fish, salt fish, let rest for 10 minutes. Combine 75g each of flour
    and rice flour with 6oz of beer (per 1lb of fish.) Let thin batter chill
    in fridge.

 2. Heat oil while chilling to 375°F.

 3. Make dredge by combining equal parts (25g) rice flour and flour. Dry the
    salted fish and dredge them, then set by oil.

 4. Coat in batter and then fry, only a few pieces (½lb) at a time, for 4
    minutes. Transfer to wire rack.

 5. Fry fries after fish is fried.
