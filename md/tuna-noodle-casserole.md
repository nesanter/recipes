# Tune Noodle Casserole

## Ingredients

 - 4 cups hot cooked egg noodles (3 cups uncooked)

 - 2 10.5 oz cans Campbell's Condensed Cream of Mushroom

 - 2 10 oz cans tuna, drained

 - 2 cups frozen peas

 - 1 cup milk, or some cream

 - (optional) something spicy

 - 2 tbsp bread crumbs, or panko

## Instructions

 1. Boil water. Cook noodles partially.

 2. Preheat oven to 400°F.

 3. Combine all ingredients except panko in a casserole.

 4. Bake for 30 minutes. Top with breadcrumbs. Bake 5 minutes.
