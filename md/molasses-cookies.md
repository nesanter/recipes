# Molasses Cookies

## Ingredients

 - ¾ cup butter
 - 1 cup sugar
 - ¼ cup molasses
 - 1 egg
 - 2 cups flour
 - 2 tsp baking soda
 - ½ tsp salt
 - spices (cinnamon, nutmeg, etc.)

## Instructions

 1. Preheat oven to 375°F.

 1. Mix dry.

 2. Cream butter and sugar. Beat in molasses and egg. Add dry and mix until
    just combined. Chill for 40 minutes.

 3. Form into balls. Bake for 11 minutes.
