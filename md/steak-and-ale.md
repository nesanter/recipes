# Steak and Ale Pie

## Ingredients

### Filling

 - 2 to 2.5lb chuck roast
 - 1 small sweet onion
 - about half of 1 bunch of relatively thin carrots
 - 1 medium-small redskin potato
 - 3 cloves garlic
 - 2 to 3 green onions, whites only
 - ½ stick salted butter
 - black pepper, coriander, and paprika
 - 3-4 bay leaves
 - 6-10 small sage leaves
 - beef BTB
 - dried bitter orange
 - 2 cans New Holland Cabin Fever brown ale
 - flour

### Crust

 - 2½ cup flour
 - 2 stick salted butter
 - pinch (½ tsp) salt
 - ⅔ cup water

## Instructions

 1. Preheat oven to 300°F.

 2. Dice onion fairly small. Cook in dutch oven with ½ stick butter, coarsely
    ground black pepper, coriander, and a few dashes of paprika, until starting
    to brown.

 3. Add carrots cut into small pieces, potatoes cut small as well, and garlic
    roughly minced. Chop the green onion. Add all to pot and cook a bit.

 4. Add two cans of the brown ale, a spoonful of beef bouillon, a few bay
    leaves, some fresh sage, and about 2 tsp of bitter orange. Bring to a
    simmer.

 5. Toss beef in flour to coat and then add to pot. Return to a bare simmer,
    then put in oven, mostly covered, for 2 hours.

 6. Forty minutes before the stew comes out, combine 2½ cups flour, 2 sticks of
    butter, and a pinch of salt in a food processor. Pulse a bit. Then, run
    while streaming in about ⅔ cup water until a ball forms. Refrigerate.

 7. When the filling comes out, check for salt, then cover it and let it start
    to cool. Raise the oven to 350°F while you roll out half the dough and
    then blind bake it for 15 minutes, docked and with weights (i.e. beans)
    on some parchment.

 8. Let the blind baked bottom crust air out for a minute while you roll out
    the other crust, then fill it and put the top on. If desired, wash with
    an egg wash (1 egg plus 1 tbsp milk or water) and then bake for 45 minutes.

 9. Let cool to solidify, or serve immediately if it doesn't need to be pretty.
