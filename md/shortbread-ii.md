# Shortbread

Use a 1:2:3 ratio, by weight, of sugar, butter, and flour, plus a pinch of salt
and cream slowly added until a dough forms.

Roll out to about 3/4" thick, flouring lightly to prevent sticking, and cut
into a rectangle. Place in pan (e.g. 9x9 pan if using 2 sticks of butter) and
dock with a fork. Cut into pieces as desired before baking.

Bake for 20-25 minutes at 350°F, until no more than slightly browned.
