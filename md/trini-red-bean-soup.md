# Trini Red Bean Soup

## Ingredients

### Green Seasoning

 - bunch cilantro
 - 1 stalk celery
 - 1 head garlic
 - 4 green onions
 - 1 bunch thyme
 - 1 wax pepper
 - ¼ cup water
 - splash of vinegar (or rice vinegar)
 - salt

### Beans

 - 1 cup red beans
 - 3-4 cloves garlic
 - thyme
 - salt
 - ~6 cups water to start

### Soup

 - Seasoned bean mixture
 - 2 tbsp vegetable oil
 - 1 tbsp brown sugar
 - half a small onion
 - 3-4 cloves garlic
 - half a tomato
 - 1 stalk celery
 - ¼ cup pumpkin
 - 2-3 decent carrots
 - thyme
 - 1 to 2 tbsp tomato paste
 - 1 tbsp chicken BTB
 - paprika
 - generous ¼ cup green seasoning
 - 2 tbsp butter
 - salt and pepper
