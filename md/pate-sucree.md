# Pâte Sucrée

## Ingredients

 - 8 oz (2 sticks) butter, soft but cool, cut
 - 170g sugar
 - 1 egg
 - 1 egg yolk
 - 425g flour
 - 8g salt
 - 30g cream
 - 15g vanilla extract

## Instructions

 1. Combine flour and salt in one bowl, and cream and vanilla in another.

 2. Beat sugar and butter for 5 minutes with paddle. Halfway, scrape down.

 3. Work in the whole egg and then the egg yolk. Scrape down after each.

 4. Add flour and paddle on low until crumbly. (About 30 seconds.)

 5. Add cream and mix until dough comes together. (About 15 seconds.)

 6. Divide into equal portions and shape into flat, round disks.

 7. Refrigerate for 2 hours, or freeze.

 8. Roll out and shape to pan. Refrigerate again for 1 hour.

 9. Blind bake at 300°F with weights and foil for 35 minutes. Let cool before
 filling.
