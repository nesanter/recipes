# Black Bean Tofu

## Ingredients

 - 1 block silken tofu, cut and salted

 - ~¼ cup douchi

 - 2 tbsp shaoxing wine

 - water

 - pinch sugar

 - ¼ tsp white pepper, ground

 - 1 tsp ground green szechuan peppercorns

 - ~1 tbsp chinese red pepper, medium coarse

 - 2 tsp light soy sauce

 - 2 tbsp minced ginger

 - 1 to 2 tbsp scallion plus more to garnish

 - drizzle sesame oil

 - black sesame seeds

 - cooked rice to accompany

## Instructions

 1. Preheat oven to 350 to 400. Cut tofu into squares and dry slightly and
    spray with oil. Bake for 30 to 60 minutes.

 2. Mix douchi with about ¼ cup water, wine, sugar, white pepper to rehydrate

 3. Heat a bit of oil in a pan. Briefly cook ginger and scallions. Add the
    bean mixture and simmer briefly. Add tofu slices, szechuan peppercorns, and
    sprinkle with chili. Bring to a simmer.

 4. Bake at 250 while the rice cooks, checking to add water as needed to keep
    from burning.

 5. Simmer off any excess water. Serve atop rice, topped with sesame oil and
    seeds and more minced scallion.
