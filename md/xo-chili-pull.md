# Pulled Noodles with XO Sauce and Chili

## Ingredients

 - 4 bundles of Shanxi pulled noodles
 - ¼ cup XO sauce
 - 2 tsp chili oil solids
 - 2 tbsp soy sauce
 - 2 tbsp mirin
 - 2 tbsp sugar
 - Black sesame seeds, to garnish

## Instructions

 1. Combine XO sauce, chili oil solids, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar in a small
    pot. Cook over medium low for 5 minutes until thickened. Set aside.

 2. Boil water. Cook noodles for 5 minutes. Drain and return noodles to pot.

 3. Add sauce mixture and stir noodles to coat. Server garnished with sesame
    seeds.
