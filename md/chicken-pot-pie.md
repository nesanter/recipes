# Chicken Pot Pie

## Ingredients

### Chicken Stock

 - 3lb chicken feet, membrane removed
 - 8 cups water (plus more as it reduces if needed)
 - 1 to 2 tbsp salt
 - some coarse white pepper
 - splash of dry marsala

### Chicken

 - 1 chicken
 - 15g salt
 - 5g sugar
 - 5g garlic powder
 - 5g finely ground black pepper

### Filling

 - 1 stick butter
 - 1 onion
 - 3 to 4 cloves garlic
 - 115g celery
 - 115g carrot
 - 75g leek
 - 75g fennel
 - 115g flour
 - 150g dry sherry
 - 1 tbsp chicken BTB
 - stilton cheese
 - splash cream, if desired
 - 450g to 900g (2 to 4 cups) chicken stock
 - 140g frozen peas
 - black pepper, thyme, rosemary, bay leaf
 - salt

### Crust

 - 2 pie crusts
 - 2 eggs (egg wash)
