# Seafood Gumbo II

The other seafood gumbo recipe makes a brothy soup with a fish stock. This one
makes a thicker dark stew for serving over rice.

## Ingredients

 - Rice to serve gumbo over

### Vegetable Base

 - 1 stick butter
 - about a ¼ cup flour
 - Several good dashes each paprika + smoked paprika
 - 1 medium onion
 - 1 generous tbsp tomato paste
 - 1 generous tbsp beef btb
 - 4 to 6 good stalks of celery
 - 4 tomatoes on the vine plus the vine
 - 6 to 8 cups or so of water

### Seafood

 - ½lb shrimp
 - 1lb mussels, shell on
 - ¼ to ½lb bay scallops

## Instructions

 1. Make a very dark roux with the butter and flour.

 2. Add paprikas. Add finely chopped onions, tomato paste, and beef BTB. Cook
    briefly, stirring frequently to avoid scorching.

 3. Add finely chopped celery and diced tomatoes. Add the tomato vine. Add
    water.

 4. Cook loosely covered on low heat for about an hour.

 5. Add seafood and return to a simmer. Serve over rice.
