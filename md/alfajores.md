# Chocolate-dipped Alfajores with Dulce de Leche

## Ingredients

### Cookies

 - 200g flour
 - 300g cornstarch
 - 2 tsp baking powder
 - ½ tsp baking soda
 - 200g butter
 - 150g sugar
 - 3 egg yolks
 - 2 tsp vanilla extract
 - ½ cup unsweetened shredded coconut

### Filling

 - 1 can sweetened condensed milk

### Coating

 - 1 cup chocolate chips
 - 2 tbsp coconut oil
 - 1 tbsp lard

## Instructions

 1. Put sweetened condensed milk in a pot where it can be well covered by
    water. Boil it with a 3 hour timer, adding water when the level gets near
    the can.

 2. While it boils, combine flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and soda and
    set aside.

 3. Paddle together butter and sugar until fluffy. Beat in yolks and vanilla
    until just combined.

 4. Add flour mixture and beat until just combined.

 5. Form dough into ball, adding a bit of cream if needed to help it come
    together. Flatten into a slight disk and refrigerate for 2 hours.

 6. Preheat oven to 350°F. Take dough out of fridge, divide, and let warm up a
    bit. Roll out each portion and cut into small rounds. Place on foil-lined
    baking sheet. Bake for about 12 minutes, until slightly browned.

 7. Let cool, then transfer to rack to cool completely.

 8. When the can has boiled, cool it by adding cool water until it is cool
    when held. It is now safe to open.

 9. Put dulce de leche on bottom of one cookie, then press into another. Roll
    the filled pair in shredded coconut and set aside. Repeat for each cookie.

 10. Melt chocolate ingredients in a double boiler. Dip one half of each cookie
     pair into the chocolate, then set on foil or parchment. Chill for 30
     minutes to set.
