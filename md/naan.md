# Naan

*This is a double batch; it makes 12 naan.*

## Ingredients

 - 540g flour
 - 24g yeast
 - 2 tsp salt
 - 2 tsp sugar
 - handful of fenugreek leaves
 - 4 tbsp melted butter plus more for brushing
 - 140g yogurt
 - 280g warm water

## Instructions

 1. Mix and knead.

 2. Rise 60 minutes.

 3. Preheat grill on low.

 4. Divide into balls. Cover and proof 20 minutes.

 5. Increase temp to medium-low for about 4 minutes.
 
 6. Flatten and grill one by one for about 90 seconds a side.

 7. Brush with melted butter.
