# Buttermilk Biscuits II

## Ingredients

 - ½lb butter
 - 425g flour (ideally chinese bun)
 - 8g salt
 - 15g baking powder
 - 3g baking soda
 - 300g buttermilk

## Instructions

 1. Mix dry. Cut up butter into ½" cubes. Toss with flour. Cut in with pastry
    cutter.

 2. Add buttermilk. Fold until combined. Dump out onto mat. Shape into
    rectangle. Roll out and letter fold 3 to 4 times, then book fold 1 to 2
    more times as elasticity permits, with quarter turns between each and
    additional flour halfway through.

 3. Wrap with plastic and rest 10 minutes in fridge.

 4. Roll out into square. Trim edges with floured bench knife. Cut into 9
    pieces. Arrange on a baking sheet. Freeze for 20 minutes.

 5. Pre-heat oven to 425°F.

 6. Brush tops with melted butter.

 7. Bake for 20 to 24 minutes, turning once.
