# Chili Cheese Fries

## Ingredients

### Chili

 - oil
 - 1 lb ground pork
 - strong pinch salt
 - bit of baking soda
 - 1 can chili in adobo
 - 1 to 2 tsp msg
 - ¼ to ½ cup water
 - strong amount of cumin
 - bit of ground coriander
 - some cracks of pepper
 - bit of garlic powder
 - ~¼ cup sour cream

### Cheese

 - 1 can evap milk
 - 1 bag 4 cheese ex

### Fries

 - Fries

## Instructions

 1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Heat oil.

 2. Blend adobo chilies, MSG, water, and spices.

 3. Cook and break up pork mixed with salt and baking soda until starting to
    clump. Add chili blend and mix. Bake for 40 minutes.

 4. Stir chili and cover. Heat oven and bake fries (to the more-done side.)

 5. A few minutes before fries come out, mix evap milk and cheese and heat
    until thickened. Combine with chili in pan, folding together.

 6. Serve on fries with a dollop of sour cream.
