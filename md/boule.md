# Boule

## Ingredients

### Poolish

 - 150g flour
 - 150g warm water
 - tiny pinch yeast

### Dough

 - 250g warm water
 - 2g yeast
 - 300g poolish
 - 12g salt
 - 450g flour

## Instructions

### Poolish

 1. Combine ingredients in deli container. Stir. Let sit 24 hours.

### Dough

 1. Combine ingredients and mix until reasonably well combined. Use dough hook
    to finish combining (4 to 5 minutes.)

 2. Ferment 30 minutes.

 3. Strength building folds (about 7.) Round off several times.

 4. Ferment 30 minutes.

 5. Repeat folds and round off.

 6. Ferment 60 minutes.

 7. Shape boule. Tension seam side. Line the bowl with a floured (mix of AP and
    whole wheat) kitchen towel. Proof seam side up for 80 minutes.

 8. Thirty minutes before proofing is done, put combo cooker in oven and
    preheat to 480°F.

 9. Dust preheated cooker with semolina. Flip dough out into it. Score. Bake
    for 20 minutes with lid.

 10. Remove lid. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes. Cool on a wire rack.
