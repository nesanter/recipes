# Cornbread

## Ingredients

 - ½ cup unsalted butter
 - 1 cup all-purpose flour (or, bread flour)
 - 1 cup polenta
 - 1 tbsp sugar
 - ½ tsp salt
 - 1 tsp baking soda
 - 2 eggs
 - 1½ cup buttermilk

## Instructions

 1. Melt butter.

 2. Whisk together flour, polenta, sugar, baking soda, and salt in bowl.

 3. Add buttermilk and eggs, mixing until combined.

 4. Add slightly cooled butter and mix until just combined.

 5. Pour batter into greased baking pan.

 6. Bake at 400°F until cornbread begins to brown (about 20 minutes.)
