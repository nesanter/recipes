# Taco Sauce

## Igredients

 - Mix of chiles (e.g. 3 pasilla, 2 guajillo)
 - Small onion (or quarter of a large)
 - 4 to 5 garlic cloves
 - Salt
 - Spices (cumin, coriander, oregano)
 - Lard

## Instructions

 1. Seed and stem chiles. Quarter onion (unless already quartered.) Combine in
    pot, cover with water, and bring to boil.

 2. Turn off heat and let sit for 20 to 30 minutes.

 3. Strain out solids, keeping liquid.

 4. Add garlic, salt, spices. Add about ¾ cup of the liquid. Blend. Add more
    liquid to reach desired consistency.

 5. Heat lard in pan until about to smoke. Add sauce. Stir and simmer uncovered
    for 5 to 10 minutes.
