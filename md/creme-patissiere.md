# Crème Pâtissière (Pastry Cream)

Makes enough to fill one tart.

## Ingredients

 - 455g milk
 - 1 tsp vanilla extract
 - 115g sugar
 - 30g cornstarch
 - ¼ tsp salt
 - 4 egg yolks
 - 2 tbsp butter, cut into ½" cubes

## Instructions

 1. Prepare ice bath, strainer, and bowl that fits in it.

 2. Combine cornstarch and sugar. Whisk together.

 3. Add egg yolks. Whisk until pale and light.

 4. Combine with milk and vanilla in pot. Whisk constantly over medium-low heat
    until thickened (~175°F), about 10 minutes. Then whisk another minute or so
    until bubbling.

 5. Transfer from pot to bowl and place in ice bath. Cover immediately with
    cling wrap. Transfer for fridge for 30 minutes.

 6. Remove from ice bath. Finish refrigerating for 90 minutes.

 7. Whisk again immediately before using to “wake up.”
