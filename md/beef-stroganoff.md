# Beef Stroganof

## Ingredients

 - 1 onion
 - 2 tbsp butter
 - 1 tbsp beef BTB
 - 1lb beef skirt
 - 2 tbsp tomato paste
 - 1-2 tbsp worch
 - salt
 - black pepper, thyme, sage, rosemary, tiny pinch cayenne
 - 2-3 tbsp flour
 - ¾ to 1 cup vermouth
 - 2 cup water
 - ~⅓ cup sour cream
 - egg noodles

## Instructions

 1. Salt both sides of the skirt

 2. Cook onions in button with black pepper, salt, and cayenne, until softened,
    about 5 minutes

 3. Add BTB and tomato paste. Cook off.

 4. Add herbs. Cook briefly.

 5. Pat skirt dry. Cut up and add.

 6. Add worchestershire, vermouth, and flour. Mix to combine. Add water.

 7. Bring to a simmer, then bake at 325° for 90 minutes.

 8. Let cool somewhat and mix in sour cream. Serve with egg noodles, letting
    them finish cooking by absorbing a bit of the sauce.
