# Beef Stroganof

## Ingredients

 - 1 onion
 - coarse black pepper plus a tiny pinch of cayenne
 - salt
 - 2 tbsp butter
 - 1 tbsp beef BTB
 - 2 tbsp tomato paste
 - a bunch of fresh sage leaves; alternatively, dried thyme, sage, and rosemary
   (about one to two teaspoons each, whole)
 - a few bay leaves
 - 2 tbsp worchestershire sauce
 - roughly ¾ cup dry white vermouth e.g. Noilly Pratt
 - 1lb (optionally, up to 2lb) beef skirt
 - 2-3 tbsp flour
 - 2 cup water
 - ~⅓ cup sour cream
 - 10 oz egg noodles

## Instructions

 1. Salt both sides of the skirt

 2. In a dutch oven, cook onions in butter with black pepper, salt, and
    cayenne, until softened, about 5 minutes

 3. Add BTB and tomato paste and mix thoroughly. Cook off.

 4. Add fresh or ground herbs. Cook briefly. Add worchestershire and vermouth.
    Deglaze.

 5. Pat skirt dry. Cut up and add. Mix to combine. Add water without covering.
    Sift on flour, stir to combine, avoiding lumps.

 7. Bring to a simmer, then bake uncovered at 325° for 90 minutes.

 8. While cooling, boil water and cook egg noodles until slightly underdone.
    Add sour cream and stir in, then add egg noodles and let them finish
    cooking for about ten minutes.
